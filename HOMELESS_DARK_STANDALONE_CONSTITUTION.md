### §82.1 Telemetry grammar and calculus [C]

Define a versioned telemetry language with `event_id, world_tick, monotonic_time, entity, observer, build, source_revision, policy_digest, metric, units, value, uncertainty, sampling_policy, causal_parent, evidence_kind`. Separate observations, derived estimates, decisions and verified commits. Metric operators include windowed count/rate, duration, latency quantile, error/residual, threshold/hysteresis, budget utilization and conservation residual. Declare units, sampling bias, missingness, confidence assumptions and alert semantics. This is a project telemetry calculus, not a claim of a new mathematical theorem. Batch measurements with bounded overhead, aggregation/retention tiers and persistent incident traces.

For each observer-object pair of interest distinguish `frustum_candidate`, `line_of_sight_clear`, `render_visible`, `perceptually_salient` and `aware`. A CPU frustum or ray test proves only its own predicate; occlusion/culling/LOD and on-device image evidence determine stronger claims. Use render instrumentation or ID/depth-buffer sampling on a graphics-capable worker/device where needed, with asynchronous readback and overhead benchmarks. Apparent rendered area, distance, contrast, screen position and dwell are explicit inputs; awareness still depends on permitted sensing and policy, not on a camera ray alone. Human attention cannot be inferred as certainty.

Emit `enter_view`, `exit_view`, `occluded`, `revealed`, `noticed`, `ignored` with hysteresis and stable IDs. An object can react to being observed only if its authored sensing capability permits that knowledge; camera visibility alone does not grant an ordinary object knowledge of a human gaze. Queue reactions to the next admitted tick, with hysteresis/cooldown and causal depth limits to prevent recursive oscillation. Hidden information stays scoped. Do not log a full observer×entity cross product; use spatial broadphase, causal interest and bounded subscriptions. Verify against controlled scenes with known occluders, camera matrices, actor poses and truth masks. Julia/Octave references validate geometric/statistical computations; device render captures remain necessary for appearance. Numerical telemetry cannot certify beauty or replace human playtesting.

### §82.2 Memory and Oracle service budgets

C# is the main Unity gameplay/adapter language, with portable reference kernels retained behind contracts. Managed memory reduces some manual-memory hazards but does not remove allocations, GC pauses, native resource lifetimes or ownership bugs. Use composition/ECS selectively, pooling and allocation profiling rather than deep inheritance as the universal entity model. Never instantiate one MonoBehaviour or neural model per planetary entity. Oracle hosts durable world/event services, caches and model workers only after actual disk/RAM/network capacity is measured. Keep off-host backups and recovery procedures; a free-tier label is not a permanence or uptime guarantee.
